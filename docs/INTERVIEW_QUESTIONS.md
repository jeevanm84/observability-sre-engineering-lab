# Interview Questions and Scenarios

## Fundamentals

Distinguish metrics, logs, traces, profiles, SLI, SLO, SLA, and error budget.

## Intermediate

Calculate burn for a 99.9% SLO at 1% errors. Explain cardinality and cross-signal correlation.

## Advanced

Design multi-window alerting, tail sampling, and missing-telemetry behavior.

## Production and Senior SRE

Diagnose collector drops, a one-region latency regression, and a tenfold cost spike. Then design multi-region telemetry that survives backend loss, enforces tenancy, and connects error budgets to release policy.
